Abstract

This project post-trains a 4B base model, Qwen3.5-4B-Base, into a tool-using financial agent that looks up market prices, macroeconomic series and company filings, computes over them in a Python sandbox and answers multi-step questions. The official post-trained Qwen3.5-4B already solves 84.7% of our earlier market-retrieval development tasks, so we built a harder offline dataset of 3,148 tasks: 2,850 generated in the style of FinSearchComp over a fixed data snapshot, with answers computed by programs and rechecked by an independent implementation, plus 98 FinSearchComp and 200 DocFinQA test tasks. A Qwen3.8-27B teacher solves the market training tasks of two difficulty tiers in an agent harness; these filtered trajectories, with filtered wealth-management and machine-learning trajectories and 2,000 general conversations, train the 4B model with multi-turn agent SFT. On 300 test market-data tasks, on held-out stocks and, for indices, ETFs, FX and FRED series, later periods, the SFT model solves 88.7%, against 57.7% for the official Qwen3.5-4B, 16.7% for the base model and 94.7% for the teacher. Reinforcement learning with GRPO from the SFT model is running now.

Collects financial question-answering sources and general SFT data (Tülu 3), synthesizes document QA, removes overlap with the test sets, deduplicates, and mixes financial and general data at set ratios.

100,000 SFT samples per run 1:1 finance and general mix

A SQLite snapshot (through 2025-10-30) of daily prices with splits and dividends, FRED macro series and SEC XBRL filings, with lookup tools and a sandboxed Python. Programs generate tasks that need many periods of data, price adjustment, unit conversion and multi-step aggregation, and compute their answers. Stocks are split by ticker and indices, ETFs, FX and FRED series by time, so test tasks use held-out stocks or later periods.

3,148 tasks 565 securities 68 FRED series 516 companies

An append-only context, so each market-data trajectory is exactly what the model saw; a checklist tool; answer verification that rejects numbers with no support in the tool results; and correction messages after rejected actions.

Market trajectories from the Qwen3.8-27B teacher pass up to 11 filters, and wealth-management and machine-learning trajectories pass the 7 that apply to them. With 2,000 general conversations they train Qwen3.5-4B-Base, with the thinking of accepted turns in the loss and tool outputs out of it.

GRPO in verl continues from the SFT model, with ablations on training tasks screened by the SFT model's pass rate, the number of updates per step, and DAPO-style dynamic sampling.

Each model runs once per task in the same harness and programs grade the answers. Models are compared with a paired bootstrap over tasks.

Half general data keeps math ability during financial SFT

On Qwen3-4B-Base with 100,000 SFT samples per run, finance-only SFT lowers GSM8K by 30.4 points below the base model, while a 1:1 mix of financial and general data stays within 1.1 points of it and loses 1.5 points of financial score against finance-only SFT. The general data includes GSM8K-style training tasks, so part of this gap comes from practice on similar tasks.

Status and Limits

  • GRPO is still training: the first run starts from the SFT model, and three ablation runs and their evaluations follow it. No GRPO result is reported yet.
  • Same task types: market-data test tasks share their types with the training tasks; only the stocks or time periods differ.
  • Task selection: the test task types were chosen where the official model did poorly on development tasks.
  • Uncertainty: each test task ran once, so the intervals reflect which tasks were sampled, not training randomness.
  • Stricter grading: a stricter regrade changed 10 answers in the market-data evaluations and left the conclusions of the comparisons of the SFT model with the base and official models unchanged.
